AntiqueFishingRod.obj is a derived exhibit version: converted to OBJ, resized to approximately 2.2 metres, pivot moved to the base, and recoloured with aged bamboo, cork and brass tones. This is an artistic vintage-style adaptation, not a scan of an authenticated historic artefact. Retain this credit when redistributing.
