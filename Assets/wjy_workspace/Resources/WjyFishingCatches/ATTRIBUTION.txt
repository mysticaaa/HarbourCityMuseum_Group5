## Changes and use

Original downloaded GLB files are retained in `Source~/`. Unity-ready meshes, textures and materials are in `Prepared/`; prefabs are in `Assets/wjy_workspace/Resources/WjyFishingCatches/`. Modifications: static bind pose extracted, coordinate conversion for Unity, texture resolution limited to 1024, PBR materials adapted to Unity Standard, and scale/origin adjusted for the fishing hook. No original animation is used. The whale is a miniature representation.

The Coca-Cola name and logo belong to their respective owner; the asset creator is Yanez Designs. This project does not claim endorsement.

When redistributing the project or player, include this attribution notice and the linked license.

`Source~` is ignored by Unity to avoid a second GLB import; the original downloadable files remain available on disk. The crab display floor was removed; presentation rotations, double-sided surfaces and miniature scale were adjusted.
