E-F01
Relayed wording: 小字看不清
Theme: legibility of exhibit instructions.
Requirement: useful prompts at the normal visitor approach position.
Priority: high impact, moderate effort; blocks independent use.
Change: common opaque, high-contrast fixed cards; readable fixed type scale;
separate action, interpretation and status; suppress only the replaced legacy hints.
Repeat task: approach, read the first instruction and explain the next action without help.
Evidence to collect: actual standing/seated distance, device, reading errors/help and participant explanation.
Remaining limit: rendered fit, occlusion and headset legibility not yet verified.

E-F02
Relayed wording: 不知道如何操作头盔
Theme: action discoverability and purpose.
Requirement: explain inspection, release-to-return and an understandable observation goal.
Priority: high impact, moderate effort; uses the existing exhibit rather than adding a prop.
Change: instructions respond to held/released state; a window-function self-check gives
explanatory retry, completion and reset. A non-holding path remains available.
Repeat task: inspect or read the helmet, explain the window's role, answer, release and reset.
Evidence to collect: first attempted action, assistance, mistakes and explanation of the feedback.
Remaining limit: self-check completion is not proof of learning or physical window detection.

E-F03
Relayed wording: 自动讲解容易误触发
Theme: intent, interruption and control over audio.
Requirement: start when the visitor intends to attend, avoid overlap and allow skipping.
Priority: high impact, higher implementation effort; affects multiple stations and transitions.
Change: fixed visit regions, entry/exit hysteresis, 1.2-second dwell and forward sightline,
nearest eligible station, once-per-visit state, Replay/Stop and automatic-audio preference.
Video/other speech defer E narration; menus, boarding, cabin and inactive gallery suppress it.
Repeat task: pass a station, pause facing it, stop, replay, open/close menu and leave/return.
Evidence to collect: unintended starts, interrupted speech, repeated triggers and user expectations.
Remaining limit: thresholds and real exhibit distances need headset and novice-user tuning.
