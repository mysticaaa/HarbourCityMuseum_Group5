Phase 4 E stability and presentation changes
Date: 28 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai)
Base: main e1fa231
Scope: E-owned scripts and tests only. No new interaction exhibit or fishing feature.
No presentation script, slides or video was produced in this change.

1. Clear controls
Cards show Listen before first use and Replay afterwards, explicit Auto audio: ON/OFF,
and distinct playing, stopped, interrupted, completed, media-busy and muted states.
When outside the visit region the message asks the visitor to move closer; unavailable
buttons are disabled rather than silently accepting clicks. Text remains available
without narration. Temporary focus/menu interruption does not reset a visit and
therefore does not unexpectedly restart the same introduction.

2. Local audio ownership
Ordinary sound effects are no longer classified as narration. E yields to video and
known NarrationPlayer speech in the same gallery, plus explicitly registered speech
sources. Additional speech can be configured on CSYGalleryGuides.additionalNarration.
E does not stop or pause those sources; it stops only its own AudioSource.

duckableMusic defaults to an empty list. Consequently E changes no other module's
volume by default. If an owner explicitly supplies same-gallery looping music,
duck/restore affects only that list. A later volume adjustment by the owner is respected.
Sounds in other scenes are neither speech blockers nor duck targets.

3. Input and lifecycle protection
Card callbacks recheck visible/enabled state, actual range and current modal state.
Helmet answers/reset recheck the same context. Menu/tutorial blocking is checked at
callback time, not only during collider updates. Disabled E cards hide their Canvas
and colliders immediately. Pausing or losing focus stops E speech and restores only
the volumes E actually adjusted.
